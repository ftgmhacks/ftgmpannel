Order Status Guide

What Your Order Status Means

You will see one of these badges next to each order in your History page.

Pending

Your order is received and waiting to start.

Processing

The order is being prepared for delivery.

In Progress

Delivery has started and is running now.

Completed

Your order was delivered successfully.

Partial

Only a part of the quantity was delivered.

Cancelled

The order could not be completed and was cancelled.

Refunded

The charge for the order was returned to your wallet.

Approved

For deposits: your payment was approved and added.

Pro Tips

Get The Best Results

A few simple habits that avoid mistakes and keep your orders smooth.

Do

  • Make sure your profile or post is public before ordering.
  • Paste the full link starting with https://.
  • Check the price, min / max and description of the service first.
  • Upload a clear screenshot and enter the exact amount for manual deposits.
  • Wait for an order to finish before placing another on the same link.

Don't

  • Don't order on a private account or a wrong link.
  • Don't enter a quantity below the service minimum or above the maximum.
  • Don't expect a cancel, edit or refund after an order is processed.
  • Don't send manual payments to any account other than the ones shown on the Add Funds page.
  • Don't share your account details with anyone.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum and maximum deposit?
Both Auto Pay and Manual Pay start from Rs. 30, and the maximum per deposit is Rs. 50,000.
Which payment methods are available?
Auto Pay works with JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast QR and is added to your wallet instantly. Manual Pay uses the active accounts shown on the Add Funds page (such as Easypaisa and SadaPay) and needs admin approval.
How long does manual approval take?
Manual deposits are normally reviewed within 15–30 minutes. For instant top-ups, use Auto Pay.
My auto payment shows “Payment Not Found”. What now?
If the payment was not completed, no funds are deducted. If money left your account but your wallet was not updated, message the main admin on WhatsApp with your payment details and we will check it.
Can I cancel or refund an order?
Once an order is successfully processed it cannot be edited, cancelled or refunded. Refill is available only for services marked “Lifetime Refill” or “30 Days Refill.”
What are “Other Services”?
They are fixed-price digital products such as premium subscriptions, software, APIs and source codes. After purchase you get an instant download button or a WhatsApp delivery button.
